Diagnosis

Root-Cause Loss Mechanisms

SEM/EDS microscopy determines whether silver loss is caused by un-settled bullion prills or dissolved chemical species.

Key Insight 70–80% of current silver losses are driven by Mechanical Entrainment of un-settled bullion prills due to short hold times and high slag viscosity.
⚙️

1. Mechanical Entrainment

Microscopic bullion prills remain physically suspended in viscous slag and fail to settle before tapping.

Share of Loss:70–80% (Dominant)
Primary Fix:Settling hold time & launder redesign
🧪

2. Chemical Dissolution

Ionic silver dissolves into silicate melt as an oxide/sulfide complex at high FeO and oxidizing redox.

Share of Loss:15–20%
Primary Fix:Reductant dosing & flux basicity
💨

3. Volatilization

Volatile Ag sub-species vaporize in high-temp zones and report to baghouse flue dust.

Share of Loss:< 5%
Primary Fix:Tuyere blast profile balancing
🔬

Phase 1 Protocol: SEM / EDS Slag Profiling

Duration: 1–2 Weeks
Scenario A: Visible Bullion Prills

SEM confirms suspended metallic droplets → Mechanical Entrainment dominates. Immediate focus: settling stage, hold duration (45 min), and launder fluid dynamics.

Scenario B: Homogeneous Matrix

SEM shows uniform Ag dispersion without prills → Chemical Dissolution dominates. Immediate focus: FeO reduction, flux basicity (CaO/SiO2 = 1.15), and collector pool availability.

Two-Track Implementation Plan

Track A physical fixes close the 192 → 25 ppm gap. Track B digital twin sustains long-term target compliance.

TRACK A: Metallurgical Fixes

Drives 192 → 25 ppm

Direct physical levers that alter phase equilibria, settling residence, and slag rheology to eliminate loss at the furnace source.

  • 1
    Phase 1 — Diagnosis (1–2 wks): Slag SEM/EDS confirms entrainment vs dissolution split.
  • 2
    Phase 2 — Quick-Win Trials (4–8 wks): Settling hold time, reductant dosing, basicity fluxing.

    Phase 3 — Process Fix (3–6 mo): Settling/cleaning stage, redesigned launder runner, feed blending.
  • 4
    Phase 4 — Hold Target (Ongoing): SOP enforcement and control limits for sustained ≤25 ppm.
